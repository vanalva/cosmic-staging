Project note

Zilker · Day 1

Why this paint failed

Three things under the old coat on the south wall:

  1. Bare wood at the trim, never primed
  2. Water getting in behind a cracked caulk line
  3. Fresh paint over chalky paint, not washed first
  • Related DIY: Why is my paint peeling? →

Quick tip

Glossy? Prime it first.

Paint slides off shine. Scuff it, clean it, prime it, then paint.
