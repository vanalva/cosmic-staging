HERE FOR THE LONG HAUL.

Cosmic is an owner-led Austin property-services company built on skilled work, clear communication, and personal accountability.

Roberto

Roberto blends an architect’s eye with a builder’s discipline. He started in commercial construction in Venezuela, moved into single‑family spec homes while completing a Master of Architecture at UT, then managed large residential projects and later served as an owner’s representative for Austin ISD, delivering and renovating K–12 schools. He also holds bachelor’s degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Industrial Engineering, which shape his process‑driven approach and attention to detail.

Estrada

Originally from Caracas, Venezuela, Carlos pairs a strong business foundation with a personal touch. He earned a B.B.A. and an MBA (Madrid), then built deep experience in home services as a top‑performing Senior Project Manager and sales leader. After seeing too many companies put profits before people, he co‑founded Cosmic to do it differently—customer experience first, every time.
